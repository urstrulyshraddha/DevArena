Faculty Guidelines & Operational Instructions

1. Evaluate After Lab Slot Closes

Trigger automated evaluation only after student submission deadlines end. Avoid clicking the evaluate button multiple times while a batch is already running.

2. Do Not Alter Gradebook Headers

Please do not edit, rename, or delete the initial columns (Registration Number, Student Name, Task Scores) in your linked Google Sheet. Custom attendance or extra notes can be added in new columns to the far right.

3. One Lab Track at a Time

To keep grading smooth and accurate, evaluate one track section at a time. If a section has more than 60–70 students, grade them in smaller groups.

4. Direct Access to Student Code

Every student submission is preserved in your Google Drive folder. You can inspect any student's original code anytime via the "View Code" links in your gradebook.

🛠️ Notice any grading inconsistencies, incorrect rubrics, or platform glitches? Send feedback to the administrator.
